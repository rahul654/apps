Apps

A small collection of web apps.

  • 💸 SplitwiseSplit expenses with friends and groups. Data stays in your browser.
  • 🛠️ Dev ToolsJSON, Base64, JWT, regex, cron, UUID, hashes, diff and converters. Runs locally.
  • 🗂️ File ToolsEdit, merge and split PDFs, pull text from images and scans (OCR), compress images, make and scan QR codes. Files stay on your device.
  • 🎥 RecorderRecord your screen with a webcam bubble, trim and download. Take screenshots and highlight, mark up or blur parts of them. Everything stays on your device.
  • 🛡️ SandboxOpen websites in isolated frames with control over scripts, storage, popups and device access. Phone/desktop sizes and an HTML playground.
  • 🪄 Background RemoverRemove image backgrounds with AI that runs in your browser. Swap in a colour, blur or photo, touch up with a brush, export PNG. Images stay on your device.
  • 🎙️ Speech to TextTranscribe or translate audio, video and mic recordings with Whisper, right in your browser. Edit with synced playback, export TXT, SRT, VTT or JSON. Audio stays on your device.
  • ✏️ WhiteboardHand-drawn whiteboards and diagrams: templates, Mermaid to diagram, PNG/SVG export. Boards stay in your browser.
  • 📡 API ClientPostman-style API client: collections, environments, pm.* scripts, collection runner, JSON compare, latency tracking, lossless Postman import/export and an optional local proxy. Data stays in your browser.
