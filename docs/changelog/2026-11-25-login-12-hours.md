# Logins last 12 hours

Summary: the site password gate and the Files upload login now last 12 hours instead of 1 hour.

## Behaviour

- Before: a login lasted 1 hour, in both the gate and the Files upload login.
- After: 12 hours. The Apps Scripts sign 12-hour tokens and the pages cap a session at 12 hours.
- The Files header shows the time left as hours and minutes ("12 h", "1 h 12 min"); under an hour it stays "52 min".

## Files

- `gate/apps-script.gs`, `files/apps-script/Code.gs`: `SESSION_MS` = 12 h (token expiry and the `ttl` returned).
- `gate/core.js`, `files/src/lib/session.ts`: `SESSION_MS` = 12 h (the client-side cap on the returned `ttl`).
- Lock screen / login dialog / header texts and the "expired" messages say 12 hours.
- `files/src/lib/format.ts` `formatMinutes`: hours and minutes from 60 min up.

## Gotchas

- The pages cap the session at `min(ttl, SESSION_MS)`, and the token's expiry is set by the script. Until both `.gs` files are pasted into their Apps Script projects and deployed as a new version, logins still last 1 hour.
- Existing 1-hour sessions keep their old expiry; the next login gets 12 hours.

## History
